## ENTRY
**Category:** Run your mystery through this
**Name:** Fair-play audit
**Description:** Confirm every clue the detective uses in the reveal was actually shown to the reader beforehand.
**Items:**
  - List every fact the detective cites in the final reveal, one line each
  - For each fact, find the earlier page where it was shown to the reader
  - If a fact appears for the first time in the reveal, either plant it earlier in the draft or cut it from the solution
  - Check that the culprit had a genuine, demonstrated opportunity to commit the crime as described
  - Check the detective's method is consistent with how they solved earlier cases in the book

## ENTRY
**Category:** Run your suspects through this
**Name:** Suspect viability checklist
**Description:** Every named suspect should hold up as a plausible culprit until the moment they're cleared.
**Items:**
  - Does this suspect have a real, independently sufficient motive?
  - Does this suspect have a genuine opportunity, not just proximity?
  - Is there at least one piece of true evidence that looks bad for them?
  - Is their eventual innocence explained by something other than the detective simply asserting it?
  - Could a careful reader have suspected this person seriously, not just in passing?
